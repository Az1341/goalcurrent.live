PROBLEM
The homepage does not make today's football across supported competitions its primary experience. Scores and Premier League match hubs exist, but the homepage underuses them and gives promotions priority. Ahmad requires live scores, today's results and upcoming games, competition grouping, and a clear dedicated match-hub link on each card.

USER BENEFIT
A visitor can immediately understand what is live, see the current score and match status, find today's finished/upcoming matches, and open the correct match hub in one action on desktop or mobile.
